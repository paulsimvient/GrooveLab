Lil God Projector v2.8.2

- VIEW is now a direct show/hide toggle for the floating mixer; no popup menu/dialog.
- Floating mixer is user-opened and remains modeless, application-level, and shared across GRID/EUC/SONG/BEAT.
- Added global REC button to the top transport bar so MIDI can be recorded while working in EUC/notes mode as well as SONG.
- R toggles recording from any page.
- REC follows the currently persistent selected instrument/channel.
- Mixer continues to expose per-track sends to Paradise, Century, Compressor, Galaxy and Capitol plus FX return/master.
