Lil God Projector v3.2.2 — Mixer Fit

- Compact mixer now targets a console-sized ~880 x 238 window.
- Migrates oversized compact mixer bounds saved by older builds.
- Compact width is clamped to 760–940 px and height to 220–270 px.
- Four channel strips are narrower and denser.
- Smaller title/send fonts, shorter send rows, smaller buttons/readouts.
- Compact master/FX-return bus narrowed and shortened.
- Detail mode remains available and retains its independent geometry.
