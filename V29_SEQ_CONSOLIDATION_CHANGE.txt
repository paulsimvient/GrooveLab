Lil God Projector v2.9 — SEQ consolidation

- Removed GRID from the primary navigation.
- Renamed EUC to SEQ.
- SEQ is now the single composition/sequencing workspace.
- STEP / EUCLID / HYBRID remain inside SEQ.
- Melodic tracks keep the split piano-roll + rhythm workspace.
- Drum tracks keep the full rhythm workspace and drum parameters.
- SONG and BEAT remain separate because they serve arrangement and performance roles.
- VIEW renamed to MIXER; clicking it directly shows/hides the persistent modeless floating mixer.
- Persistent instrument selection continues across SEQ / SONG / BEAT / MIXER.
- Legacy GRID implementation remains internally for compatibility but is no longer user-visible or navigable.
