Lil God Projector / GrooveLab v2.0

- Record quantize defaults to 1/16.
- Added OVERWRITE recording mode: notes recorded onto an occupied quantized step replace notes already starting on that step.
- Added per-instrument MIDI-lane MUTE.
- Added per-instrument Euclidean gating in Piano Roll: EUCLID, Steps, Pulses, Rotate.
- MIDI-lane mute and Euclidean settings are stored in project/song/take data.
- Improved Mini-Moog discovery for UADx naming and auto-load from the in-app keyboard.
